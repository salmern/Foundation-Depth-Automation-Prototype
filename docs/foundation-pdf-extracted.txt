Heave (Traditional Foundations)
The heave zone indicates the area influenced by vegetation, where it is
necessary to provide a suitable compressible material against the internal side of
external foundations.
Minimum void dimension against the side of the foundation to comply with NHBC
chapter 4.2.10 Table 7.
The actual thickness of material to be installed to achieve minimum void
dimension, should be established from the manufactures recommendations.
Where void formers are required against the side of foundations concrete pours
should be limited to the maximum height permitted by the void former
manufacturer. This is typically 2.5m but the BBA certificate relating to the specific
product should be checked to confirm this.
No heave protection is required for internal footings or where the NHBC is
satisfied that the soils are not desiccated. The heave zone also indicates where it
is necessary to provide either a voided suspended floor such as beam & block or
a insitu suspended floor with a suitable void former.
Voided floor slabs or suspended slabs with a suitable void former will be required
if the surface soils are desiccated (NHBC Standard Chapter 4.2.10).
